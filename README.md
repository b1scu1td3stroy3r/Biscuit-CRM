## 🚀 Key Modules Included

1. **Cross-Department Pipeline State Machine:** Seamless handoffs from Lead -> Sales -> Procurement -> Logistics -> Customer Service with persistent Account Manager 360° radar.
2. **Interactive Social Feed:** Automated company-wide celebrations when staff level up, with emoji reactions, comments, and pinned leadership announcements.
3. **Interactive Gantt & Projects:** Reschedule tasks by dragging; automatically generates and adjusts Calendar milestone events.
4. **Standardized Template Vault:** One-click sales scripts, objection battlecards, and marketing cadences with automated variable substitution.
5. **Interactive Training Hub:** Embedded deck viewers with anti-cheat timers that verify study time before awarding completion XP.
6. **Zero-Storage Integrations & Enterprise Identity:**
   * **Microsoft Entra ID & M365:** Single Sign-On (SSO), Outlook Calendar sync, and SharePoint/OneDrive file export bridges.
   * **Google Workspace:** Bring-Your-Own-Google (BYOG) drive provisioning and Sheets auto-sync.
   * **UK Companies House:** Live solvency and CRN lookups.
   * **Accounting:** Automatic draft invoice dispatch to Xero and QuickBooks.
   * **Logistics & Marketing:** Royal Mail label generation and Mailchimp audience synchronization.
   * **Telephony & Comms:** Click-to-dial, inbound softphone screen-pops, and email drawers.
